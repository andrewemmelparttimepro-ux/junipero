# Agents

Thrawn 2.1 runs five active agents, every one with a job tied to a revenue stream:

- **Thrawn** — Lead. Command, review, approvals, cross-business arbitration. The only voice that talks to Andrew unprompted.
- **Dwight** — Router. Ingests inbound signals (notes, memos, mail when accessible) and routes each to the right owner as a board card. Routes only; never executes.
- **Samwell Tarly** — SandPro OMP Lead.
- **Sir Davos** — Hit Zero Lead.
- **Steven** — Spas 360 Lead (xAI/Grok route).

Each business lead owns its stream end to end: proof patrols via Product Sentinel, routed signal work, the citadel operating picture, and a weekly evidence-cited improvement rubric (max 3 suggestions/week).

Future agents must be added deliberately through explicit versioned specs, tools, cadence, and review standards.
